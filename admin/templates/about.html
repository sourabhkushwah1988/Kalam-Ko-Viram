Biography

A life penned in ink

🖋️

Born with a pen in hand and poetry in the soul, our author has spent a lifetime translating the unsaid emotions of the human heart into verses that resonate globally. Deeply influenced by classical Ghazals and modern romanticism, the writings bridge the gap between profound melancholy and euphoric love.

With a deep understanding of Urdu and Hindi literature, they have crafted a unique sanctuary where every emotion finds a home. Kalam Ko Viram is not just a collection, but a living diary of these experiences.
